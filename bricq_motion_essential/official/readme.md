# LEGO® Education BricQ Motion Essential Official Lessons Index

A complete index of official LEGO® Education BricQ Motion Essential lessons organized by unit. Designed for elementary school students (Grades K–5), these hands-on, non-tech lessons use sports contexts to explore physical science principles like push and pull forces, friction, momentum, and energy transfer.
