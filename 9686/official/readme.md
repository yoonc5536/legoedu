# LEGO® Education Simple & Powered Machines Official Lessons Index

Designed for middle school students (Grades 6–8), these hands-on lessons explore physical science, mechanical engineering principles, structural design, pneumatics, and renewable energy.

| **Structures** | 30-45 min | Beginner | 6-8 | Investigate frame structures, triangulation, and internal/external forces | [View Lesson](https://education.lego.com/en-us/lessons/spm/structures/) |
