# LEGO® Education Simple & Powered Machines Official Lessons Index

A complete index of official LEGO® Education Simple & Powered Machines (Set 9686) lessons organized by unit and extension module. Designed for middle school students (Grades 6–8), these hands-on lessons explore physical science, mechanical engineering principles, structural design, pneumatics, and renewable energy.
