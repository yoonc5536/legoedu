# LEGO® Education BricQ Motion Prime Official Lessons Index

A complete index of official LEGO® Education BricQ Motion Prime lessons organized by unit. Designed for middle school students (Grades 6–8), these hands-on, non-tech lessons use sports contexts to explore physical science principles like Newton's laws of motion, force vectors, potential and kinetic energy transfer, momentum, and mechanical advantage.
